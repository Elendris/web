Welcome

Looking for a calm and comfortable place to rest during your business week or weekend in Brno?

Elendris – Guesthouse near the church is the ideal choice for both business stays and family trips. We are located in the quiet district of Brno-Slatina, just minutes from the airport and the D1 motorway.

On-site parking
Outdoor seating and kids trampoline
Complimentary coffee and tea
Shared kitchen for guests
Public Transport Stop 30 Meters Away (trolleybus 31)

We offer online check-in. If you pay in advance, we will send you an access code and you can arrive at any time, even during the night.
Check-in is available from 3 PM to 8 PM. Check-out is by 10 AM.

Our rooms

All our rooms are air-conditioned and have a private bathroom. Family rooms are equipped with a bathtub. Breakfast can be delivered directly to your room at a time that suits you.

High-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the property. You can use the shared kitchen with fridge and freezer. Coffee and tea are available free of charge all day. The entire property is non-smoking.

If you have any questions, do not hesitate to contact us by email info@elendris.cz

Where can you find us?

You can find us in the Brno‑Slatina district, a few minutes' drive from the D1 motorway and Brno‑Tuřany Airport.
